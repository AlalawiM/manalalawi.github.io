01

About

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the University of Hail. My academic work brings together research, university teaching, student mentoring and academic service, with a particular interest in scalable and distributed computing.

I hold a PhD and an MSc in Computer Science from the University of Bedfordshire, and a BSc in Computer Science from Taibah University.
